## Notes from autocomplete
- "SDR" collides with IMF currency, software-defined radio, HDR video. Titles must say "SDR (sales development)".
- BDR is mostly a US/UK term; in India people say SDR.
- Freshness modifiers are common: "since yesterday", "in the last 3 days", "in the last week".
- Company salary searches exist: rippling sdr salary bangalore, salesforce / hubspot / databricks / browserstack bdr salary india, gitlab / datadog / canonical / atlassian sdr salary india.
